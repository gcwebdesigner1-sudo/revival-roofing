Questions

Straight answers, no pressure.

How do I know if I need a repair or a full replacement?

We do a thorough, documented inspection and give you an honest answer. If a repair solves the problem for less, that's what we recommend. If the roof is at the end of its life or damaged all over, we lay out the replacement options with clear pricing.

How much does a new roof cost in North Idaho?

Every roof is different. Material, pitch, complexity and square footage all factor in. After a free inspection you get a detailed, itemized estimate with no hidden fees, so you know exactly what you're paying for.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential re-roofs take 1 to 3 days depending on size and weather. We protect your property the whole time, clean up daily, and keep you posted at every step.

Do you work with homeowners' insurance for storm damage?

Yes. We document storm damage thoroughly, can meet your adjuster on site, and help with the paperwork so the claim goes as smoothly as possible.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Revival is a licensed contractor in Idaho (#6171541) and Washington (#REVIVRE751BU), and fully insured.
