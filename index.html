How it works

No pressure.
No surprises.

Homeowners keep saying the same thing in reviews: the bid was the most thorough they got, and nobody pushed. Here's how a job goes.

Book a free inspection
  1. 1

    Free inspection

    We get on the roof and into the attic. A lot of roofers skip the attic. That's where the rot and leaks hide.

  2. 2

    A bid with photos

    You get pictures of every problem we found and a plain explanation of what we'll do about each one. Then we walk you through it.

  3. 3

    Install and daily cleanup

    Most homes are done in a few days. The crew picks up nails and debris at the end of every day, not just the last one.

  4. 4

    Final walk-through

    We come back, check the work with you, clean the gutters and fix anything you're not happy with.

About

A North Idaho native who does it right the first time.
