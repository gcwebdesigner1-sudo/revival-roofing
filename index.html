Questions

Straight answers, no pressure.

How do I know if I need a repair or a full replacement?

We perform a thorough, documented inspection and give you an honest assessment. If a repair solves the problem cost-effectively, that's what we recommend. If the roof is at end-of-life or broadly damaged, we'll lay out the replacement options with clear pricing.

How much does a new roof cost in North Idaho?

Every roof is different — material choice, roof complexity, and square footage all factor in. After a free inspection we provide a detailed, itemized estimate with no hidden fees so you know exactly what you're paying for.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential re-roofs are completed in 1–3 days depending on size and weather. We protect your property throughout, clean up daily, and keep you informed at every step.

Do you work with homeowners' insurance for storm damage?

Yes. We document storm damage thoroughly, meet with your adjuster on-site, and handle the paperwork so the claim process is as smooth as possible.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — Revival Roofing and Exteriors is fully licensed and insured. Our Idaho registration is #6171541 and our Washington license is REVIVRE 751 BU.
